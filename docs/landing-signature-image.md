Create a premium, believable architectural photograph of a beautifully designed contemporary Iranian boutique entrance at blue hour. Main subject: large exquisite brushed stainless dimensional Persian channel lettering with soft warm-white halo illumination on deep navy blue architectural fascia. Exact Persian text, clearly legible: «تابلوی تو». No other writing. Pale cool limestone, finely ribbed metal, a slender glass entrance, subtle interior light, one tasteful plant, refined reflections. This is the photograph itself, not a website mockup, no buttons, no watermark, no people, no exaggerated orange cast.
